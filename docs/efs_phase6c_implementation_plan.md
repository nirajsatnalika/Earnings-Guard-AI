# EFS™ PHASE 6C — FREE-FIRST PEER & INDUSTRY INTELLIGENCE PLAN

This document outlines the architectural plan for building the **Free-First Peer & Industry Intelligence Layer** for EarningsGuard™ AI.

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails
1. **Frozen EFS Methodology:** The 95 variables, 7 pillars, 110 forensic rules, 5 established models, calibration logic, and snapshot hashing remain 100% frozen.
2. **100% Free / Public Data:** NO paid market APIs (no Bloomberg, Capital IQ, FactSet, Refinitiv, AlphaSense). Use public filings, stock exchange disclosures, and SEC EDGAR.
3. **No Guessing or Fabrication:** Unavailable data returns `EXTERNAL_DATA_UNAVAILABLE` with clear provenance—never guess or zero-fill.
4. **Supporting Evidence Only:** Peer intelligence provides external context; it does NOT alter EFS pillar scores or engine rules.
